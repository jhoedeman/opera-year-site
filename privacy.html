Privacy Policy

An Opera Year · Effective November 17, 2026

An Opera Year doesn’t collect any information about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that does.

What the app keeps, and where

The app remembers your favorites, your ratings, your settings (such as your listening buddy, the appearance and your music services), the day your listening year started, and your reminder time. It keeps them on your device. It also notes the day your free month began, so it knows which of your first 30 pieces are free.

If you’re signed in to iCloud, the app syncs most of these between your iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud account, so your favorites and ratings follow you. Apple stores that data under its own privacy policy, and the developer can’t see it. Without iCloud, everything stays on your device.

Deleting the app removes what it kept on that device, except the day your free month began. That date stays in your device’s keychain, where iOS keeps it after the app is deleted, so reinstalling finds the same free pieces. It’s a date and nothing else. What the app synced may stay in your iCloud account.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store and handled entirely by Apple, under Apple’s privacy policy. The app never sees your payment details or your Apple Account. It only asks Apple which of its purchases you own, so it can unlock them on each of your devices. The developer gets Apple’s usual sales reports, which don’t name you.

Reminders

iOS schedules the daily reminder on your device. Nothing about it is sent anywhere.

Listening and sharing

A listen button opens the day’s piece in Apple Music, Spotify or another music service you’ve chosen. That service’s own privacy policy covers what you do there. The app tells it nothing about you; the link only names the recording.

When you share a piece, the text goes only where you choose to send it.

Feedback

Feedback is an ordinary email from your mail app. The developer sees your email address and your message, and uses them only to reply.

Children

The app is for listeners of every age, and it collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions

To ask about this policy, open the app’s Settings and tap Send Feedback.
