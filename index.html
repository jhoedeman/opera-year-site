An Opera Year

Support

One opera piece a day, for a year. Each day brings a piece to hear, the story behind it from Giuseppe, and what to listen for from your listening buddy.

Getting in touch

Open the app’s Settings, from the gear on the Today tab, and tap Send Feedback. Questions, problems and suggestions are all welcome.

Listening

A day’s piece has a button for each music service you’ve checked in Settings, under Music services, in the order you’ve put them there. Drag a service to change its place.

The daily reminder

Turn on Daily reminder in Settings and pick its time. If it doesn’t arrive, notifications may be off for An Opera Year: Settings says so and offers a way to iOS Settings to turn them on.

Using an iPhone and an iPad

Your favorites, ratings, buddy, music services and your year sync between devices signed in to the same iCloud account. There’s nothing to set up beyond iCloud itself.

Your year

Your year starts the first day you open the app, which becomes Day 1. To begin again, tap Start My Year Over in Settings; your favorites and ratings stay.

Your free month and purchases

Your first 30 pieces are free, and they stay free. The rest of your year is a one-time purchase, with no subscription. A purchase follows your Apple Account to your other devices and, with Family Sharing, to your family. If a purchase doesn’t show, tap Restore Purchases in Settings, under Keep listening. To use an offer code, tap Redeem Code there.

Seeing the introduction again

Tap Replay Intro in Settings.
